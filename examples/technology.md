# Technology recommendation examples

## Fast-moving topic

**User**

> Recommend books about artificial intelligence for someone who works in technology but is not an ML researcher.

**Expected behavior**

Because AI changes quickly, explicitly evaluate whether books are current, partially dated, timeless, or historically important but dated. Do not rank a book highly solely because it was influential several years ago.

Balance conceptual foundations, practical understanding, history, economics/societal implications, and appropriate technical depth. Exploration picks could connect AI to decision theory, cognitive science, organizational change, philosophy of science, or technology history.

## Technical progression

**User**

> I want to move from general AI understanding into how modern language models work. Give me a reading path rather than ten independent recommendations.

**Expected behavior**

Switch to reading-path mode. Order material by prerequisite and progression, explain why the sequence matters, and identify material that can be skipped depending on the reader's mathematical/programming background. Do not force the default ten-book presentation when a structured progression better serves the explicit request.
