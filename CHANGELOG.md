## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-05

### Added
- Initial public release.
- Understand -> Discover -> Investigate -> Diversify -> Rank -> Present -> Learn workflow.
- Maximum of three clarification questions.
- Broad multi-source and international candidate discovery.
- Candidate-pool-first research to reduce confirmation bias.
- Active criticism and contrarian-view research.
- Edition intelligence and verified-content safeguards.
- Default ten-book recommendation format.
- 80% preference-match / 20% exploration model.
- Top-three deep-dive behavior.
- Book-cover guidance when supported by the active interface.
- Confidence-weighted reader preference graph using available ChatGPT memory/context.
- Explicit, behavioral, inferred, and exploration-reaction preference signals.
- Positive/negative preferences, recency, contradiction handling, and time decay.
